Scope & interpretation

This snapshot screens for candidate sections. It cannot show the share of drivers speeding, individual violations, or persistent hotspots. A section average does not locate speeding inside a smaller speed-limit zone.

Exclusions

A section can have multiple exclusion reasons; these counts overlap.
