# ITSC 2026 Constraints And Track Fit

Last checked: 2026-02-23.

## Submission constraints (official)

- Initial paper length: up to 6 pages for regular, invited-session, and industry papers.
- Accepted final version: up to 2 extra pages can be purchased (max 8 pages total).
- Format: standard IEEE conference format.
- Review model: regular and industry papers are double-blind; each paper gets at least 2 reviewers.
- Submission system: PaperPlaza (linked from ITSC author page).

## Key date for your current planning

- Regular and invited-session paper deadline: 2026-03-01.

## Why this fit

- The method centers on spatial indexing, tiled map assets, and on-device geospatial query systems.
- The ITS contribution is deployment impact (ISA/speed-limit support), not traffic-control policy optimization.
- That aligns better with data/infrastructure/validation-oriented ITS tracks than pure control-theory tracks.
