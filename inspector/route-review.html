How to read the comparison

Inspector sign groups use the same IDs, Panoramax membership, and position proposals as Zeichen vor Ort. A crop-reviewed route group can establish a sign class outside those snapshots while its physical position remains open. Unassigned evidence includes automatic associations that did not resolve to a sign group.

Crop-reviewed cases use the visible device crops, manually annotated images or historical classified images. “No saved read” means the journal has no matching observation during a comparable passage. The journals do not provide continuous camera footage.

Automatic rows use class, camera position and heading. “No matched read” flags a candidate for review when a moving GPS fix was within 22 m of a same-direction reference photo. Nearby or opposite-facing signs, changed signs, camera angle and repeated captures can affect matching. WFS positions reuse the previous Inspector estimates. Other positions can be estimated from a photo box or represent the camera position.

Historical evidence is associated using camera position and direction. Counts include repeated views. A manual Panoramax class edit requires matching sign geometry and label in the mirrored edit history.
