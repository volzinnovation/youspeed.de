Details and conditions
  • After measurement correction, fines start at +4 km/h for limits up to 120 km/h. At a 130 km/h limit, enforcement starts at +1 km/h.
  • The amount depends on corrected excess, road type and roadworks. Fees are additional; no single amount applies to this whole band.
  • Above +30 km/h, criminal handling generally applies. On motorways, that threshold is +40 km/h. The amount and further sanctions depend on the case.
  • From +40 km/h, criminal handling also applies on motorways. The fine and possible licence sanctions are determined case by case.
  • At +50 km/h or more, police seize the licence when stopping the driver. The prosecutor or court decides how long it is held; no fixed fine is assumed.
