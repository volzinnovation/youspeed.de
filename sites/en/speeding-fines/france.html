Details and conditions
  • Standard fine: 135 EUR at limits up to 50 km/h, otherwise 68 EUR. No French licence point deduction.
  • Standard fine: 135 EUR at limits up to 50 km/h, otherwise 68 EUR. 1 French licence point deducted.
  • Standard fine: 135 EUR; 2 French licence points deducted.
  • Standard fine: 135 EUR; 3 French licence points deducted. Court-ordered licence suspension of up to 3 years is possible.
  • Standard fine: 135 EUR; 4 French licence points deducted. Court-ordered licence suspension of up to 3 years is possible.
  • French criminal offence; 6 points deducted. A 300 EUR fixed settlement may apply; court fine up to 3750 EUR. Licence sanctions are possible.
