## Parked checks and driving scope

Android's actual dashboard and Fahrtenbuch were exercised on the phone. Fahrten
is immediately left of Settings with the same 48 dp circular style. History,
empty state, date presets/custom bounds and photo/video filters opened without
errors. Automatic trip saving remained off; no consent was accepted for the
owner. Startup logs were retained through the existing Keep-and-continue action.
Real parked GPS produced 0 km/h, explicit 30 km/h on a residential road and
qualified urban context. No position was injected.

iPhone's normal app installed and launched successfully, and a final readback
confirmed version 1.5/build 10060, unchanged preferences and unchanged active-map
metadata. After the owner unlocked the Mac, QuickTime showed the attached
iPhone's actual Fahrtenbuch screen: the local-journal explanation, automatic
trip-saving switch in the off position, consent explanation and search field
were visible. A screenshot is retained below. No control was changed and no
QuickTime recording was started. This establishes that the physical journal
screen renders; its history filters and recording flow were not exercised.
Read-only CoreDevice inspection also confirmed that the normal 1.5/build 10060
application was running after unlock, matched to its own installed executable.

Earlier, one isolated, read-only UI-test attempt timed out enabling automation
before any test body ran, and reopening the normal app was denied while the
iPhone was locked. These earlier checks remain in their receipts; the subsequent
QuickTime observation resolves the visual-inspection blocker. No security
setting was overridden, and the test host did not replace the normal application.

No recording/upload controls were enabled, and no simulated location, trip
deletion or mounting change was initiated. Parked launch/history checks do not establish a physical
DE:310/DE:311 crossing or a lower-sign preview during a real drive. Those remain
the owner's road test. The automated implementation coverage is recorded in the
