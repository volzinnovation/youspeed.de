# Android Dashcam screenshot replacement

The genuine-road Dashcam replacements are uploaded and saved as drafts in Google Play Console for nine locales: `de-DE`, `en-US`, `es-ES`, `fr-FR`, `it-IT`, `nl-NL`, `pl-PL`, `pt-BR` and `sv-SE`. Every locale was revisited after saving: each gallery has eight phone screenshots, with Dashcam second. The seven other images retain their original Google asset URLs. No review submission or publication was performed.

[Remote verification](remote-verification.json) records the saved gallery order, uploaded filenames and local file hashes. [German](de-DE-gallery.png) and [English](en-US-gallery.png) gallery screenshots show the saved layouts; [the uploaded-image proof](android-dashcam-upload-verified.png) shows the actual Google-hosted Dashcam replacement.

The 1920 × 1080 opaque RGB files are in `store/android/listing/<locale>/phone-screenshots/`. Their Fastlane mirrors are byte-identical. [Composite validation](../dashcam-composites-2026-10-03/validation.json) confirms all nine output hashes, mirror equality and preservation of native controls and pixels outside the camera pane.

The road pixels come from 5.5 seconds of a verified iPhone Dashcam recording. [Source provenance](../../../store/artwork/dashcam-preview.json) records the original video hash, private archive revision, frame hash and crop. The crop removes the recording car’s mirror, dashboard and hood; the reviewed crop and final views contain no visible people or cars. This is a requested marketing composition with retained native demonstration UI, rather than a live screenshot of that recording session. Raw native screenshots remain unchanged.

[The existing Play visual report](../PLAY_CONSOLE_VISUAL_VALIDATION_2026-10-03.json) records the nine-locale gallery inventory and order before these replacements. The saved draft changes remain pending review and publication.
