# Panoramax privacy and account readiness — 2 October 2026

This is a read-only audit of the two server choices shipped by both apps. It resolves published facts, not unpublished server operations or submitted store answers. Anonymous HTTPS GETs inspected the landing pages, deployed client source, public configuration, legal pages, OpenAPI descriptions and login redirects. No token was generated, account created, login completed, claim followed, message sent, photo uploaded or data deleted. The accompanying JSON records public response hashes and status codes; temporary authentication-session parameters are excluded.

## Account deletion applicability and actual routes

Google's requirement includes app-directed external account creation. The OSM France path creates a contributor account on first login and offers external signup. **Recommended applicability: yes, as an evidence-based policy interpretation for this shipped flow.** Core driving being account-free does not establish an exemption. A bare connection to a pre-existing third-party identity would need a different assessment; that description is incomplete here. Final Console answers still require owner review. [Google account-deletion scope](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en-EN).

Google permits a deletion-request web resource reached from the app; it must cover the account and associated collected data and identify YouSpeed/the developer. The current Disconnect control only revokes the current token, and local cleanup only removes device files. The published contacts have not been exercised or verified as a complete account-and-upload deletion service. [Google User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en).

Apple also covers external-browser account creation and expects an in-app initiation path. An ordinary email-only support route is insufficient for an app outside the highly regulated exception. Content associated with the account is included; an open-data licence alone does not prove a required-retention exemption. **Recommended readiness: unresolved on both platforms until the instance contributor-account scope and an appropriate deletion flow are reconciled.** [Apple account-deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/).

Both live OpenAPI documents advertise authenticated deletion of collections/photos and token revocation. They advertise `GET`/`PATCH` for `/api/users/me`, with no whole-account `DELETE` route. The existence of content endpoints is not proof that the apps expose or have exercised deletion. The apps presently call only the token-revocation deletion route. [YouSpeed OpenAPI](https://panoramax.youspeed.de/api/docs/specs.json), [OSM France OpenAPI](https://panoramax.openstreetmap.fr/api/docs/specs.json), [iPhone account implementation](../../iphone/SpeedConsumerApp/PanoramaxAccount.swift), [Android account implementation](../../android/app/src/main/java/de/youspeed/android/alpha/PanoramaxAccount.kt).

Deleting the external OpenStreetMap identity and deleting the OSM France Panoramax contributor record/content are separate scopes. OSM Foundation documents its own account-removal limits; that policy cannot certify Panoramax deletion. Its account request must identify affected services. [OSM Foundation account removal and privacy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy#Account_Removal).

## Store-label consequences

The four established native upload categories remain photos, precise location, user IDs and embedded user content, optional and retained/account linked. The live YouSpeed policy introduces additional reconciliation work: name/email in the account service, retained network/security diagnostics and research purposes. Native clients do not collect name/email through their UI or parse the validation profile body; login occurs in the system browser. Confirm exactly which account-service data originate from this app flow, developer access, retention and purpose mapping before adding or excluding those label categories. The existing four-category privacy manifest is not a certification of these newly inspected server practices. [Apple App Privacy definitions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/).

Moonshots is the published app controller and YouSpeed-instance publisher, but HSPF's contractual processing role is not established by the legal notice. OSM France operates an independent public contribution service. A Google service-provider exemption cannot be assumed. Selected uploads and Android onboarding explicitly describe GPS transfer for publication, supporting a user-initiated sharing-exception assessment; verify the final screens, account identifier handling and subsequent research/federation transfers before choosing a global “No sharing”. Face/plate blur does not establish full anonymization. [Google Data safety definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en).

## Exact outstanding owner decisions

1. Identify the separate YouSpeed-instance privacy policy and establish legal bases, host/controller/processor relationships, research uses, recipients, storage location and retention for account data, logs, raw/blur inputs, published content and backups.
2. Establish OSM France access-log, photo and backup retention; verify its contact aliases and account-plus-content removal procedure, separate from the OSM identity provider.
3. Provide a discoverable in-app account-and-data deletion initiation path and a functioning public YouSpeed deletion resource; verify operator execution, timing and legally required retention, then complete Console questions. No deletion requests were sent during this audit.
4. Reconcile additional data types/purposes with both store labels, manifests and the final binaries; retain unresolved answers instead of silently marking unknown handling “No”.
5. Confirm exact blur recipients and unblurred-data treatment on both deployments; do not claim that the initial uploaded JPEGs are already anonymized.
6. Make the local public privacy draft identify publication/licensing, instance contacts and server roles clearly; it remains unpublished. Reviewer account access and console privileges are separate remaining release checks.
