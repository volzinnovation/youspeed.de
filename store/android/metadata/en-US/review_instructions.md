YouSpeed 1.4 (10056) provides driving assistance, optional dashcam recording and street-photo capture. Recognition, maps and advisory penalty estimates can be wrong; road signs and traffic law take precedence. No enforcement tolerance is automatically deducted.

Review flow:
1. Complete Setup while parked. Download a regional map and the relevant recognition model in Settings / Data Manager; downloads require internet. Driving then uses the installed maps offline. Precise location while using the app supports speed and map matching. There is no persistent background driving service, and camera capture stops when inactive.
2. Start the driving view. To show ordinary controls, hold the phone face-up within 10 degrees of horizontal at 20 km/h or less. Configure the app while stationary. Dashcam recording, traffic-sign recognition and street photos have independent controls. A manual photo can be captured without stopping recording. Camera access is optional; microphone access is optional for audio and voice functions.
3. Recognition is initially disabled; enable it in Setup or Settings. Sound, spoken speed, spoken speed plus other signs, or silent feedback can be selected. Country, installed model and device affect availability and recognition quality. Supplementary-sign OCR retains text evidence but does not guarantee correct interpretation. After sunset, camera recognition pauses and installed maps supply the reference.
4. Record and stop a local dashcam clip, then inspect playback, sharing and deletion in the video library. Videos are not automatically uploaded. With photo capture enabled (the initial setting), movement with the app open captures location-tagged street photos. Review photos while parked, select them and explicitly start a Panoramax upload. No account is required for driving, recording, playback or local photo review.
5. Optional Panoramax uploads require an existing connected account and internet. Connect through the system browser in Settings; supported HTTPS servers include YouSpeed and OpenStreetMap France. Uploaded photos include GPS/time/heading and may contain sign annotations. Disconnect revokes local authorization; it does not delete the external account or server photos. Do not upload private test imagery.
6. Sign-database contributions are a separate consent-based flow. Declining permission leaves recognition available. With permission, sign observations and automatically selected crops may upload to the private database, including over mobile data. Offline crops may remain queued for up to seven days. Settings supports withdrawal and deletion requests. This is separate from explicit full-photo Panoramax publication.
7. Diagnostic logs are initially enabled and remain local until explicitly exported; disable logging in Settings if preferred. Logs and exports may contain precise location. Startup can offer removal of logs over 100 MB.
8. Info contains offline national traffic-sign catalogs and penalty tables, with source references and advisory limitations. Supported sign names can be spoken in German, English, French and Dutch. Optional on-device voice correction requires the appropriate microphone/speech permissions and language support; there is no cloud speech fallback.

There are no advertising or tracking SDKs. This is a manual-release store draft; review submission and publication are separate steps.
