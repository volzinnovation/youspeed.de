YouSpeed 1.3 is a driving assistance app. It can be wrong; road signs and traffic law remain authoritative. Penalty notices are advisory GPS estimates, with no enforcement tolerance automatically deducted. Country rules include Germany, France, Switzerland, Belgium and the Netherlands; exact amounts are shown only where the implemented rules and road context support them.

Review flow:
1. On first launch, clear or keep saved logs if prompted, then complete Setup. Select and download a map, or use location to suggest one. Downloads use public HTTPS assets and require internet. Data Manager in Settings lets you select regions, review available/installed package details and delete downloaded maps.
2. Grant precise location while using the app for the driving display. Start the driving view to inspect the speed and map-derived speed limit. There is no background location permission request or persistent background driving service. Camera capture stops when the app is inactive.
3. Camera features are optional and request permission when first started. The red recorder control starts a local dashcam recording. Video, sign recognition and photo capture have separate controls. Stop recording before opening playback, sharing, photo review or upload. The video library supports playback, sharing and deletion; videos are not automatically uploaded to Panoramax.
4. Camera sign recognition is disabled initially. Enable it in Setup or Settings. The independent option allows recognition without saving video. Feedback can be sound, spoken speed only, spoken speed and other signs, or silent. Additional-sign speech requires the recognized-sign display option. The eye marker beside a numeric limit identifies an active camera-derived reference. Country/model/device limitations apply; hundreds of classifier classes do not mean every sign is reliably recognized or can change the limit.
5. With microphone permission, local voice capture can record a speed correction. On iPhone, speech authorization and supported on-device recognition are required. Android uses a local Vosk model for German, English, French or Dutch correction capture. With the voice permissions already granted, a short listening window after a newly confirmed camera limit accepts the localized “Wrong” command to dismiss that camera reference. There is no cloud speech fallback.
6. Photo capture is enabled initially, but needs camera permission and movement with the app open. Review the location-tagged photos after the recorder stops. Select photos and explicitly start Upload to Panoramax; capture is not an upload. Uploads require a connected Panoramax account and internet. Settings supports YouSpeed and OpenStreetMap France HTTPS servers; authorization opens the system browser and connection is then verified in the app. Uploaded photos contain GPS/time/heading metadata and may include sign annotations. Disconnect removes authorization; it does not delete server photos or the external account.
7. Diagnostic logging is enabled initially and stores logs locally; switch it off in Settings. The startup storage review offers clearing saved logs over 100 MB. Local logs and exports may contain precise location and technical recognition/matching details.

No account is required for offline driving, dashcam playback or local photo review. An external Panoramax account is required only for the optional upload flow. There are no advertising or tracking SDKs.
