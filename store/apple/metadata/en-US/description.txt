A familiar road. A changing speed limit. Keep the essentials in view with YouSpeed.

Turn your iPhone into a driving companion for the car you already have. See your current speed beside a speed-limit reference, hear helpful alerts for traffic signs and record images and video of your journey using the built-in dash cam. No connection to your car is needed.

Keep speed limits and traffic signs in view
The offline data bundles and camera recognition follow speed-limit references along your drive. Receive optical and acoustic warnings according to your own preferences. The app directly shows country-specific penalty estimates give you an idea of what excessive speed could cost when you are caught by a speed cam or police.

Hear the signs
Your iPhone recognises supported traffic signs on the device. Choose a sound or spoken feedback for recognised signs. Ideal for driving in foreign lands and teaching traffic rules to young drivers.

Record now. Watch later.
The built-in dashcam can save a  video on your iPhone. Use your recordings to have evidence in accidents and to back your security, share a clip or delete what you no longer need.

Offline data
Download the traffic sign databases for your area, then use the app without an ongoing mobile connection. Maps are available for Germany, France, Switzerland, Belgium and the Netherlands and.many other countries The Data Manager helps you manage downloads and storage.

Explore and contribute
Browse offline traffic-sign catalogues and country-specific fine tables in Info. Sign names can be spoken in English, French, German and Dutch. On supported devices and in supported languages, you can record speed-limit corrections by voice. You can also capture street photos, review them and choose which to upload to Panoramax.

Your recordings, your choice
Driving features need no account. Camera and microphone access are optional. With photo capture enabled, the app automatically takes photos while moving with the app open. Full street photos and dashcam videos stay on your iPhone until you choose to share or upload them. Optional Panoramax uploads need a connected account and internet; uploaded photos include location and capture details. Local diagnostic logs are enabled by default and can be switched off in Settings. No advertising or tracking SDKs.

Set up and operate controls while parked. Recognition and map data can be incomplete or wrong. Country coverage and penalty estimates vary, and the reference tables are not legal advice. Always follow road signs and traffic rules; YouSpeed cannot guarantee the applicable limit or a legal outcome.

Optional sign contributions: with your permission, detected sign classes, scores, observation times and GPS positions are sent to the private YouSpeed sign database using a random installation ID. The daily collection plan selects cropped sign photos for automatic upload, including over mobile data; negative examples are always selected. Offline crops may wait locally for up to seven days. Withdraw permission or request deletion in Settings. Recognition works without contribution permission. This does not publish full photos to Panoramax.
