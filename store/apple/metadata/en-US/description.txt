Drive safely. Give your car a driving companion.

YouSpeed turns your smartphone into a driver assistance system for any car, whatever its age. Keep speed limits in view, hear alerts and record your drives. No built-in vehicle system required.

SEE THE SIGNS. KEEP YOUR ATTENTION ON THE ROAD.
Automatic traffic sign recognition uses your phone's camera and runs on your device. Country models cover hundreds of traffic sign classes, including speed limits and additional road signs. Choose sound or spoken feedback. Supported signs and recognition availability depend on the country and installed model.

KNOW YOUR SPEED. UNDERSTAND THE CONSEQUENCES.
See your current speed alongside the speed-limit reference. Configure sound alerts and get country-specific penalty estimates where supported, so you can understand what speeding could cost.

RECORD THE DRIVE. KEEP THE VIDEO.
Use the built-in dashcam to record drives locally. Play back, share or delete your videos when parked. You choose what to keep and share.

READY BEYOND MOBILE COVERAGE.
Download maps before you go and use the speed display without an ongoing map connection. Maps and country advisory rules include Germany, France, Switzerland, Belgium and the Netherlands.

MADE FOR YOUR CAR. BUILT AROUND YOUR PRIVACY.
Core driving features need no account. No advertising or tracking SDKs. On supported devices and languages, correct speed limits by voice. Capture street photos and review them before choosing an optional Panoramax upload.

Set up YouSpeed while parked. Camera recognition can miss or misinterpret signs; availability depends on the installed country model and device. Map coverage and penalty information vary by country. Traffic signs, traffic rules and official decisions remain authoritative. YouSpeed cannot guarantee the applicable speed limit or a legal outcome.

Core driving features do not require an account. Camera and microphone access are optional. Photos can be captured automatically while moving with the app open when photo capture is enabled. Photos and dashcam videos stay on your device until you choose to share or upload them. Panoramax uploads require a connected account, an internet connection and your selection; uploaded photos include location and capture metadata. Local diagnostic logging is enabled by default and can be switched off. No advertising or tracking SDKs.

Behind Info, browse country-specific speeding fine tables and a traffic-sign catalogue with pictograms and spoken names in English, French, German and Dutch. The reference pages are available offline and are not legal advice.
