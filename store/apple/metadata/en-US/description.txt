A familiar road. A changing speed limit. Keep the essentials in view with YouSpeed.

Turn your iPhone into a driving companion for the car you already have. See your current speed beside a speed-limit reference, hear helpful alerts and save video of your journey. No connection to your car is needed.

Keep speed limits in view
Use downloaded map data and camera recognition to follow speed-limit references along your drive. Set your own speed-alert threshold. Where supported, country-specific penalty estimates give you an idea of what an excess could cost.

Hear the signs
Your iPhone recognises supported traffic signs on the device. Choose a sound or spoken feedback for recognised signs. Available signs and camera recognition depend on the country, installed model and device.

Record now. Watch later.
The built-in dashcam saves video on your iPhone. When parked, play back your recordings, share a clip or delete what you no longer need.

Prepare once. Use maps offline.
Download the maps you need before you leave, then use them without an ongoing mobile connection. Maps are available for Germany, France, Switzerland, Belgium and the Netherlands. The Data Manager helps you manage downloads and storage.

Explore and contribute
Browse offline traffic-sign catalogues and country-specific fine tables in Info. Sign names can be spoken in English, French, German and Dutch. On supported devices and in supported languages, you can record speed-limit corrections by voice. You can also capture street photos, review them and choose which to upload to Panoramax.

Your recordings, your choice
Driving features need no account. Camera and microphone access are optional. With photo capture enabled, the app automatically takes photos while moving with the app open. Photos and videos stay on your iPhone until you choose to share or upload them. Optional Panoramax uploads need a connected account and internet; uploaded photos include location and capture details. Local diagnostic logs are enabled by default and can be switched off in Settings. No advertising or tracking SDKs.

Set up and operate controls while parked. Recognition and map data can be incomplete or wrong. Country coverage and penalty estimates vary, and the reference tables are not legal advice. Always follow road signs and traffic rules; YouSpeed cannot guarantee the applicable limit or a legal outcome.
