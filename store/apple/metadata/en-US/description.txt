YouSpeed shows your current speed and the speed limit derived from offline map data. Download supported countries and regions, manage them in Data Manager, and use the driving display without an ongoing map connection.

Offline maps and country-specific advisory rules include Germany, France, Switzerland, Belgium and the Netherlands. The camera models contain hundreds of country-specific sign classes in total, including speed limits and additional road signs. Class coverage is not a recognition accuracy guarantee; only supported signs can be displayed or affect the speed-limit reference.

Features:
- Live speed and speed-limit display with source information
- Configurable sound alerts and advisory penalty information where supported
- Optional dashcam videos saved locally, with playback, sharing and deletion
- Optional traffic-sign recognition on your device, with selectable sound or spoken feedback
- Local spoken speed-limit corrections on supported devices and languages
- Geolocated street photos for review and optional upload to Panoramax

Set up YouSpeed while parked. Camera recognition can miss or misinterpret signs; availability depends on the installed country model and device. Map coverage and penalty information vary by country. Traffic signs, traffic rules and official decisions remain authoritative. YouSpeed cannot guarantee the applicable speed limit or a legal outcome.

Core driving features do not require an account. Camera and microphone access are optional. Photos can be captured automatically while moving with the app open when photo capture is enabled. Photos and dashcam videos stay on your device until you choose to share or upload them. Panoramax uploads require a connected account, an internet connection and your selection; uploaded photos include location and capture metadata. Local diagnostic logging is enabled by default and can be switched off. No advertising or tracking SDKs.
