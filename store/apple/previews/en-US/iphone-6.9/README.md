# YouSpeed iPhone preview draft

`youspeed-app-preview.mp4` is a 30-second English preview at 1920 × 886 / 30 fps. H.264 High Profile Level 4.0, progressive yuv420p, measured 10.927 Mbps, AAC stereo at 48 kHz, target 256 kbps, and MP4 fast-start metadata. The final file is 41,955,856 bytes. Encoding checks and a full audio/video decode passed.

It uses native iPhone app captures only. Camera recognition results are explicitly labelled examples from the app's native screenshot fixtures; they do not show live inference. The draft shows mounting choices, recorder configuration, picture cadence, and recognition-result presentation. Actual camera calibration, recording interactions, and Gallery playback still need device capture for a fully filmed demonstration. Apple makes the final content-review decision.

The companion feature film includes the supplied mounted-phone photos, verified empty-road Dashcam footage, and an attributed Panoramax scene. Its [production notes](../../../../videos/feature-film/en-US/production-notes.md) document sources, exclusions, licensing, and capture limitations.

The poster, contact sheet, SRT/VTT captions, timeline, build log, and render report accompany this preview. Nothing was uploaded or submitted to App Store Connect.

References: [Apple preview specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-preview-specifications) and [App Preview guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-previews/).
