YouSpeed shows the detected speed limit and your current speed while you drive. The app uses local offline map data and provides advisory warnings when a speeding violation may become costly.

Features:
- Live speed-limit and speed display
- Offline map data for supported countries and regions
- Advisory fine, points, and driving-ban warnings
- Local spoken speed-limit capture
- No ads and no tracking

YouSpeed is an assistance app. Road signs, traffic rules, and official notices are always authoritative.
