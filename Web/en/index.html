Current app status

The website only promises what the apps can support

Public first

iPhone consumer app

Germany data, local speed-limit lookup, warning levels, on-device spoken capture, and OSM attribution are bundled into the launch path.

Android app

Shared contract

Offline bundles

Country and region packages are treated as reproducible assets. The launch remains focused without overstating Europe-wide live operation.

Live display

Warning levels that stay readable in the car

YouSpeed reduces the drive view to speed, limit, and consequence. The colors follow the app behavior: neutral, fine, points, driving ban, and unrestricted Autobahn.

What the app does

Built for orientation, not distraction

The core functions come directly from the existing app surfaces and store metadata.

01

Live speed limit

Current limit and speed sit at the center of the driving view.

02

Advisory warnings

Fine, points, and driving-ban information is guidance, not legal advice.

03

Offline maps

Map data lives locally on the device and works while driving without connectivity.

04

Local correction

Detected limits can be captured locally by voice and reviewed later.

Trust

Clear limits, open data

YouSpeed is an assistance app. Road signs, traffic rules, and official notices remain authoritative.

OpenStreetMap

Speed data is based on OSM tags such as maxspeed, maxspeed:type, and source:maxspeed.
