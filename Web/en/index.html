App features

Driver assistance for any car

Sign recognition, local recordings and offline references join the speed display and warning levels.

01

Recognize traffic signs automatically

Your phone’s camera recognizes speed limits and additional road signs on your device. Choose sound or spoken feedback. Supported signs depend on the country and installed model.

02

Record your drive with the dashcam

Keep videos locally and play them back when parked. Share or delete recordings and choose what to keep.

03

Review and share street photos

Review geolocated photos in the gallery. Optional Panoramax uploads start only after your selection and require a connected account and internet access.

04

Look up fines and traffic signs

Behind Info, browse country-specific speeding fine tables and a traffic-sign catalogue with pictograms and spoken names. Available offline; the information is advisory.

05

Manage map packages more easily

Search countries and regions, see package dates and download sizes, and download or delete maps independently. Supported countries include Germany, France, Switzerland, Belgium and the Netherlands.

06

Travel across borders

1.3 improves cross-border map switching, GPS fix handling and road-matching continuity. The app is available in English, German, French and Dutch.
