Details and conditions
  • Standard immediate settlement: 58 EUR, plus a 10.67 EUR administrative fee (2026).
  • Immediate settlement: 58 EUR plus 12 EUR per km/h beyond +10 in built-up, school, 30 km/h, residential or shared zones; otherwise 7 EUR. Add a 10.67 EUR fee.
  • Immediate settlement: 58 EUR plus 12 EUR per km/h beyond +10 in built-up, school, 30 km/h, residential or shared zones; otherwise 7 EUR. Add a 10.67 EUR fee.
  • Above +30 km/h, court referral can apply in built-up and special zones; on other roads the threshold is above +40. No single fine is assumed.
  • Above +40 km/h, court handling generally applies. The fine and possible licence sanctions depend on the individual case.
